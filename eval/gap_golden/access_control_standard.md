# Skyline Cloud: Access Control Standard

## Scope
This standard applies to all staff, visitors and external parties such as suppliers and service providers who access Skyline's network and information systems.

## Access control policy
Skyline establishes, documents and implements logical and physical access control policies for access to its network and information systems, based on business requirements and security requirements.

## Sign-in
Users sign in to company systems with a username and a password of at least 14 characters. Passwords must not be shared.
