# Northwind Analytics: Personal Data Breach Procedure

## Purpose
This procedure tells staff what to do when personal data may have been lost, stolen or exposed.

## Roles
The incident lead coordinates the response and informs senior management and the legal team as soon as possible.

## Processor duties
When Northwind processes personal data on behalf of a client, we notify that client without undue delay after becoming aware of a personal data breach.

## Information in stages
Where not all information about a breach is available at once, we provide it in phases without undue further delay.

## Breach register
Every personal data breach is recorded in the breach register, comprising the facts relating to the breach, its effects and the remedial action taken.

## Telling customers
The communications team may inform affected customers of a breach where it considers this appropriate.
